Your halwai

Ramesh Gupta, owner

My father, Dadaji's old friend Bhola ji, and I started this shop in 1994 with one kadhai and a tin counter. My elder brother Suresh would deliver boxes on his cycle before school.

I still remember our first Diwali. We sold out by 2 PM and made ladoos till midnight. People in Gandhi Nagar have trusted us ever since, and now their children come here with their own children.

I taste every batch myself. If it is not good enough for my mother's thali, it does not go on the counter.

  • Only pure desi ghee, never palm oil
  • Made fresh every morning, not stored for days
  • Sugar levels adjusted on request
  • Bulk and wedding orders taken 2 days ahead
  • Same family, same recipes, since 1994
